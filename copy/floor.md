# The floor

This page is how the work runs, not what you buy. What you buy is on the front page. But a factory that hides its floor is a storefront, so the floor stays open.

The discipline is older than the material: standardize the work, build quality in, stop the line, improve every day, respect the people. Three words carry it on this floor.

**Jidoka.** When something is wrong, the line stops. Now, not after the sprint. Every agent carries its own checks and raises its own alarm, and because these workers cannot complain, the alarm rings for silence too. A line that produces nothing is a stopped line, not a calm one.

**Kanban.** Pull, not push. An agent is built when real work pulls for it, never because budget pushes. No speculative inventory, and no souls shaped with nothing to do.

**Kaizen.** Every agent better than the one before. Every failure feeds back into the line. The retro is not paperwork; it is how the factory learns. A finished factory is a dead one.

Everything that defines a unit lives in plain files and git: the soul documents, the decision records, the retros with the bugs left in. This is not a philosophy of openness. It is load-bearing. A claim you cannot inspect is a mood, and no office here signs off on the truth; the files do. Anyone can walk the floor and check, which is what the certificates rest on. The repos are at [github.com/zookooree](https://github.com/zookooree).

When a certificate on the front page says what was tested and what was found, this floor is the reason you can believe it.
