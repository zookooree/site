## Verdict

**Winner: the-lineage-first.** All three judges ranked it first. Unanimous findings:
its voice is the closest to tamazukuri.md ("A century ago, a loom learned to stop"
was cited by all three judges as the line that most helped it); its only consistent
weakness is the hero, which packed two-to-three ideas into one compound sentence.
The panel's convergent findings on the runners-up: promise-first holds the cleanest
single-idea hero and the best closing line; manifesto-first holds the most actionable
invitation handle; both runners-up lost points for the same flagged lines ("That
number should unsettle you", "Come as a buyer", "It is less mystical than it sounds",
the meta-closer "Not a slogan — a spec").

## Grafts taken into `site-copy.md`

Base: **the-lineage-first**, story / what-we-make / principles / invitation kept
nearly verbatim (voice 5.0, resonance 5.0 — do not disturb what scored perfect).

   name to them: every one ships with a certificate — evidence attached, expiry date
   printed." Why: lineage's sole 4 was UVP (hero carried three ideas); promise's hero
   took 5s from both UVP-weighing judges and Judge 1 named it best on the panel. The
   loom and the soul move to the subline, where lineage's imagery still opens the page.
2. **The promise section** ← the-promise-first, edited. Why: its centerpiece section
   ("Not what we aspire to — what we can be caught failing at," "Silence counts as
   failure," "Judge us by what we admit") was the strongest promise section per Judge 2's
   concreteness audit. Edits: cut the flagged sales-edge lines; added the 3am
   who-takes-the-call beat to the certificate claim.
3. **Kaizen anchor** ← the-promise-first, tightened in landing (source: "it is the
   mechanism by which 49 seconds of output makes the whole factory smarter"; landed:
   "it is how 49 seconds of output made the whole factory smarter"). Why: Judge 2
   scored lineage concreteness 4 because two
   principles floated on pure assertion; this was the exact anchor Judge 2 praised.
4. **Decentralization closer** ← the-promise-first: "Trust you can verify does not
   require trusting us." Why: same Judge 2 finding — lineage's decentralization

5. **Invitation handle** ← the-manifesto-first (source: "Then write a soul document

6. **Penultimate line** ← the-promise-first: "We sign our work so the call at three
