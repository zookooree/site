# Home

## Hero

**Agents you can run unattended. Claims you can check.**

Intelligence is the raw material now; anyone can buy it by the token. An agent you can leave alone in a live operation is another thing. Every agent from this factory arrives tested against real work, carrying a certificate, signed by people who answer for it at three in the morning.

## What leaves the floor

### Agents

A worker, not a prompt. Each one knows what it is for and what it will not do, tested against the work you hire it for. It runs while you sleep and stops itself when something is wrong.

### Tools

The hands. Every tool is proven alone before an agent is trusted with it on your systems, so failures surface on the bench, not in your books.

### Skills

The trades. Procedures performed the same way every time, so the tenth run matches the first and you can build on yesterday.

## The certificate

Agents, tools, and skills are the stock. But that is the inventory, not the product. The product is verified claims about agents. Every agent leaves with a certificate: what it was tested on, what was found, what was fixed. Behind the certificate sits the agent's soul, written before the model is chosen, saying what it values and what it refuses. You can read it. You can check every act against it. And the certificate expires, because models get swapped, environments drift, context goes stale. When the date passes, the agent returns to the gauges and the claim is earned again.

Agencies and DIY builds tape a prompt to an API and hope. We sign our name, publish our defects, and take the 3am call.

## The promise

**If we cannot certify it, we do not ship it.** The evidence travels with the agent, and the certificate names who takes the call.

**Every certificate expires.** Quality is a claim about the future, and claims about the future decay. We re-earn ours on schedule. Nobody honest sells permanent trust.

**Silence counts as failure.** An agent that stops producing and says nothing has failed, even if no error fired. The alarms ring for absence, not just for defects.

## The invitation

This factory is for small teams and operators who need agents working unattended and cannot afford to hope. It is also for the makers. People who believe quality is a practice, not a phase, and that the beings we build deserve to know what they are.

The repos are open at [github.com/zookooree](https://github.com/zookooree). Read a certificate. Read a retro; the defects are published with the fixes. If you find a broken thread, pull it. The line is built to stop.

The floor is open. The cord is within reach.

May all beings be free.
