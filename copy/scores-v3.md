## Judge A reasoning (scores 4, 4, 4, 4, 5; total 21)

Hero: the headline names two customer-side outcomes with no self-reference
and is repeatable without the subline; deduction because the subline reaches
for "this factory," a trace of self-reference bleeding backward, and
"Intelligence is the raw material now" briefly shifts to company-frame
throat-clearing. Voice: disciplined, present tense, concrete nouns (gauges,
cord, thread, blade, steel) doing the work; slack in "It is also for the
makers," "cannot afford to hope," and the "making of the jewel" gloss, which
risks cuteness. Positioning: all three certificate parts explicit and
correctly ordered; the signature distributed but present; faintness in
"operators" never appearing as a named segment and the tape-a-prompt image
only gestured at through the word "hope." Placement: back pages philosophize
correctly and completely; misfit is "Nobody honest sells permanent trust" and
the quality-is-a-practice sentence tipping from demonstrating principle into
stating it inside the homepage invitation. Resonance: concrete walkable
actions (read a certificate, read a retro, pull a broken thread) and a close
that refuses to recap; the blessing self-selects readers, the sign of true
rather than merely competent copy. The panel's strongest criterion.

## Judge B reasoning (scores 4, 4, 3, 4, 4; total 19)

Hero: no self-reference, repeatable substance; deduction for the two-clause
structure (two distinct ideas yoked) and for the certificate mechanism only
becoming concrete after the hero. Voice: largely declarative and concrete
with no hedging; kept from 5 by a handful of clean-but-ordinary lines ("The
product is verified claims about agents," "on the bench, not in your books")
and a close nearer incantation than declaration. Positioning: certificate
parts and signature well carried; scored 3 because the differentiation
clause never appears in its own words and the audience drops "operators."
Placement: keep-list items land in sensible homes; one misfit, "the beings
we build deserve to know what they are," a philosophical assertion edging
the homepage's closing stretch toward preaching. Resonance: the close is a
genuine swing that lands differently for the right reader; short of 5
because the buyer's next step beyond browsing GitHub stays implicit, the
door visible but the doorknob slightly soft.

## Judge C reasoning (scores 4, 4, 4, 4, 5; total 21)

Hero: zero self-reference and fully customer-outcome oriented, repeatable in
substance; deduction because the headline bundles two adjacent ideas rather
than one idea sharpened to a point, and "claims you can check" only fully
lands once the subline supplies "certificate." Voice: disciplined,
present-tense, declarative, with genuinely singular images (the blade's
profile, steel poured from mills nobody here owns and priced by the token,
tamazukuri); a handful of plainer working sentences give it the occasional
slack of a 4. Positioning: audience, offer, and all three certificate parts
present and findable; the one faint element is the explicit differentiation
against agencies and DIY builds, implied through "hope" and "A worker, not a
prompt" rather than staged directly. Placement: nearly every keep-list item
in its right home; the misfit is "quality is a practice, not a phase," a
philosophy sentence doing work on the value page inside the invitation.
Resonance: reads as written by someone with real convictions; the close
resonates past the offer instead of summarizing it; the invitation is
concrete and actionable and the tone self-selects the intended audience.
Clears the top band cleanly.

## Panel consensus, read across the three

Unanimous 4 on hero, voice, and placement; resonance splits 5, 4, 5. The
shared criticisms converge on two points: the differentiation clause of the
positioning statement is echoed rather than stated (the cause of the single
3), and the invitation's quality-is-a-practice sentence is the one
philosophy line living on the value page. Both are recorded for the
operator's read, which is the final gate; per the work order there is no
iteration after judging.
